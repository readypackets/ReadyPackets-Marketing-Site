Frequently asked questions

Clear answers for the first questions.

ReadyPackets is designed to make a business easier to explain, prepare, and move forward. Here are answers to common questions about the work and the portal.

What is ReadyPackets?

ReadyPackets helps founders organize business information into clear, professionally prepared documentation, frameworks, and deliverables. The work is designed to make the next strategic, operational, partner, or professional conversation easier.

Who is ReadyPackets for?

ReadyPackets is for founders, entrepreneurs, operators, consultants, and project-based businesses that need clearer business documentation and an organized foundation for a next-stage decision or conversation.

What happens after I start?

You begin in the secure portal, select a starting point, and provide the relevant context through guided intake. The scope, review process, and deliverables are then organized around the work you selected.

Does ReadyPackets provide legal, tax, accounting, or investment advice?

No. ReadyPackets is not a law firm, accounting firm, tax adviser, investment adviser, broker-dealer, or substitute for licensed professional advice. Prepared materials may help you organize a conversation with a qualified professional, but you should obtain advice appropriate to your circumstances.

Where are completed materials delivered?

Completed materials are organized in the ReadyPackets portal so you can access, review, and manage the work from your account. Portal availability and access are governed by the applicable service terms and account controls.

Can I start with one packet and add work later?

Yes. Many businesses begin with the work that makes the next decision easier. A clear, completed packet can become a more efficient starting point for future work.

What data is collected on the ReadyPackets website?

The ReadyPackets website uses essential local storage/cookies for your privacy preference. Microsoft Clarity is configured for optional website analytics and does not load unless you affirmatively choose Analytics. PostHog and advertising remain unconfigured. The website does not share portal account, order, payment, document, or authenticated identifiers with analytics providers. See the Privacy & cookie notice for details.

How do I get help with an existing order?

Sign in to the portal to access order-specific information or support. Do not include sensitive order, identity, payment, or document information in a public e-mail.

Still deciding where to start?

Explore the process, review the solution areas, or begin in the portal when you are ready.
