Refund Policy

Clear refund and cancellation information.

ReadyPackets aims to be fair and transparent in refund decisions. This policy explains cancellation, partial-refund, quality-review, and request procedures for applicable orders.

Effective date: August 19, 2026. This policy is based on the ReadyPackets refund policy source provided for this release. It should be reviewed and approved by qualified counsel before reliance as final legal terms.

1. Cancellation before work begins

If you cancel an order before work has commenced, you are entitled to a full refund minus a 5% administrative processing fee. Submit cancellation requests in writing through cancellations@readypackets.com with an acknowledgement message or through the customer portal messaging system.

Work commences when a team member has been assigned and has begun research, drafting, or other substantive work on your packet.

2. Cancellation after work begins

If you cancel after work begins but before final delivery, the refund is based on completion stage recorded in ReadyPackets’ project-management records:

  • 0–25% complete: 75% of the order total, processed within 48 hours of cancellation.
  • 26–50% complete: 50% of the order total, processed within 5 business days.
  • 51–75% complete: 25% of the order total, processed within 5 business days.
  • 76–100% complete: no refund; see the quality-review process below.

The completion percentage will be communicated after the cancellation request.

3. After delivery

When a final deliverable is available for download in the portal or sent by email, the order is complete and no refund is issued for the completed deliverable. If you believe a deliverable materially fails to meet the agreed scope, use the quality-review process below.

4. The All-In bundle

For the All-In bundle, refunds are considered by phase: phases not started are eligible for a full refund, the phase in progress is considered under the completion-stage schedule above, and delivered phases are not refundable. If cancellation changes a six-packet bundle, applicable package terms are recalculated under the order terms in effect at the time of the order.

5. Quality review

If a delivered packet materially fails to meet the agreed scope in the order confirmation and intake documentation, you may request a no-cost revision to bring it in line with that scope. If the issue cannot be resolved through revision, ReadyPackets may issue a partial refund proportional to the deficient portion of the deliverable.

Submit quality claims within 14 days of delivery and include a detailed description of how the deliverable fails to meet the agreed scope. Subjective dissatisfaction, such as a preference about tone, is addressed through the included revision process rather than a refund.

6. Non-refundable items

  • Consultation fees after the consultation has taken place.
  • Rush or expedited-delivery surcharges after expedited work begins.
  • Third-party costs incurred on your behalf, including filing fees, domain registrations, and software licenses.
  • Custom add-ons that have been fully delivered.

7. How to request a refund

Use the customer portal messaging system or email cancellations@readypackets.com with the subject line Refund Request — [Order Number]. Include the order number, reason for the request, and supporting documentation. ReadyPackets will acknowledge the request within one business day and provide a decision within five business days.

8. Refund processing and disputes

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Card refunds may take 5–10 business days to appear; bank transfers may take 3–10 business days. You will receive an email confirmation when a refund is processed.

If you disagree with a refund decision, email disputes@readypackets.com with the subject line Refund Escalation. ReadyPackets will review the matter and respond within 10 business days. Unresolved disputes are subject to the applicable dispute-resolution provisions in the Terms of Use or controlling written agreement.

9. Policy changes

ReadyPackets may change this policy. The refund terms in effect when an order is placed govern that order unless a controlling written agreement states otherwise.

10. Contact

For general questions about this policy, contact compliance@readypackets.com. Use the portal or cancellation address above for order-specific requests.
