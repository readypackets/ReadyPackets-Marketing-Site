Privacy & cookie notice

Clear choices for the ReadyPackets website.

This notice explains how the ReadyPackets website handles essential preferences and optional analytics or advertising. It does not replace the separate portal privacy information that applies to authenticated portal services.

Effective date: October 22, 2026. This operational notice should be reviewed by the organization’s privacy counsel before a public launch in any jurisdiction where specific disclosures, data-processing terms, or consumer-rights notices are required.

1. Scope

This notice applies to the public ReadyPackets website at www.readypackets.com and its static pages. The ReadyPackets portal at my.readypackets.com and/or portal.readypackets.com is a separate authenticated service with separate operational data processing.

2. Essential storage

The site stores a local privacy-preference record so it can remember whether you selected optional Analytics and/or Advertising. It may also store a non-identifying appearance preference when you explicitly choose Light or Dark mode; System mode is the default and follows your device setting without storing a separate appearance choice. This storage is necessary to honor your selections and is not used for advertising profiling.

3. Optional analytics

Microsoft Clarity is configured for this public website and does not load unless you actively select Analytics. When loaded, Clarity may process website interaction and device/browser information in accordance with its provider configuration. The ReadyPackets website does not send custom account identifiers, e-mail addresses, order identifiers, document names, messages, payment information, or portal-authenticated identifiers to Clarity. PostHog is not configured; if it is enabled later, its autocapture and session replay remain disabled and it sends only the reviewed marketing-page path event.

Provider-side settings—such as data region, retention, IP treatment, masking, deletion, access roles, exports, and data-processing terms—must be configured by ReadyPackets before activation. Do not enable a provider until the notice, lawful basis, and applicable transfer safeguards have been reviewed for the relevant audience.

4. Optional advertising and sponsorships

Advertising is disabled by default. If ReadyPackets configures a direct sponsorship or a manual advertising network placement, the placement is clearly labelled and remains hidden until you affirmatively select Advertising. A direct sponsorship does not load a third-party tag by itself. An advertising network may process device, cookie, or similar online identifiers in accordance with its notice and settings. ReadyPackets does not use the public website to share portal accounts, order identifiers, file information, payment information, or authenticated portal identifiers with an advertising provider.

Where required for the audience, ReadyPackets will use the required consent-management process before serving advertising. In particular, personalized Google advertising for users in the EEA, United Kingdom, or Switzerland requires a Google-certified CMP integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. See Google’s EU user consent policy information and business data responsibility information.

5. Choices and withdrawal

You can choose Analytics, Advertising, both, or essential storage only, and you can change your preference at any time. You can also use the color-theme icon in the site header to cycle System, Light, and Dark; selecting System removes any saved appearance override and follows your device preference. Withdrawing an optional preference stops new optional website loading in the current and future visits where browser storage is available. It does not erase information a provider already processed; contact the provider and ReadyPackets as applicable for requests concerning prior processing.

6. Links to the portal

Links to the portal open a separate origin. The ReadyPackets website does not share a portal session cookie, customer record, order, file, or authenticated identifier with the portal.

7. Your rights and contact

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or information about processing. For general privacy inquiries, contact privacy@readypackets.com. Do not transmit sensitive portal or order details by ordinary e-mail.

8. Updates

ReadyPackets may update this notice as the site, providers, or legal requirements change. A revised effective date will be posted on this page. Material changes to optional analytics or advertising require a new consent choice.
