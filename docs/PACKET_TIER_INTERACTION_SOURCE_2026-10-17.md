# ReadyPackets Packet-Tier Interaction Source

**Prepared:** October 17, 2026
**Purpose:** Approved content source for the independent marketing website's price-free packet-tier interaction.

The public ReadyPackets catalogue identifies six primary packet groups and the All-In master bundle. The implementation must show names, purpose, tiers, and selected scope only. It must not show prices, discounts, payment terms, or price-derived statements.

## Idea & IP Protection

**Category:** Intellectual Property
**Purpose:** Helps entrepreneurs document, structure, and protect ideas and inventions before filing with an attorney or moving to the next development stage.

**Tiers:**

- **Basic:** Organizes an invention summary with core architecture, features, alternatives, friction analysis, and next steps.
- **Standard:** Builds on Basic with a fuller inventor packet, complex figures, alternative embodiments, risk mapping, and a standard prior-art scan.
- **Premium:** Builds on Standard with a detailed patent dossier, high-fidelity diagrams, claim-theme mapping, strategic pivots, deeper analysis, and revisions.

**Illustrative scope points:** 20–50 minute intake session; Core Invention Architecture; Core Feature List + Alternatives; Full Inventor Packet; Alternative Embodiments; Attorney-Ready Patent Dossier.

## Business Foundation

**Category:** Business Formation
**Purpose:** Foundational strategic documents for new businesses to launch with clarity and structure.

**Tiers:**

- **Basic:** A clean, executable 30-day roadmap to reduce launch-phase uncertainty.
- **Standard:** A deeper plan structuring the business build, launch, and early operations.
- **Premium:** A robust, long-range strategic dossier supporting execution and scaling.

**Illustrative scope points:** Comprehensive Intake Session; Core Business Concept Map; Core Value Proposition Architecture; Target Audience Avatar; MVP Scope; Basic Go-To-Market Strategy.

## Business Plans

**Category:** Business Planning
**Purpose:** Professional business plan documents structured for lenders and institutional stakeholders.

**Tiers:**

- **Basic:** Structured funding narrative with intake, narrative architecture, tailored executive summary, mission and value proposition, financial summary, and marketing strategy.
- **Standard:** Complete lender-ready plan adding a longer dossier, three-year financial model, market opportunity, competition, operations, management, and one revision.
- **Premium:** Full-spectrum institutional package adding a strategic dossier, capital materials, industry benchmarking, executive summary, and two revision rounds.

**Illustrative scope points:** Comprehensive Narrative Intake; Narrative Architecture; Executive Summary Tailored to Funding Goals; 1-Year Pro-Forma Financial Summary; Market Size & Opportunity Analysis; Operations & Management Summary.

## Operating Design

**Category:** Operations
**Purpose:** Operational frameworks and systems design documents that give businesses the structure to scale efficiently.

**Tiers:**

- **Basic:** A clean operational framework documenting how the business runs day-to-day.
- **Standard:** A structured operational playbook enabling delegation, consistency, and scalable growth.
- **Premium:** An enterprise-grade operations architecture built for rapid scaling and institutional review.

**Illustrative scope points:** Core Operations Architecture; Core Process & Workflow Map; Basic Role & Responsibility Matrix; Detailed Process Documentation; Organizational Chart & Staffing Plan; Advanced KPI Dashboard Architecture.

## Execution & Partnerships

**Category:** Execution & Growth
**Purpose:** Bid packets, partnership frameworks, and execution documents for contractors, consultants, and project-based businesses.

**Tiers:**

- **Basic:** A professional bid packet supporting clear project scope and documented project planning.
- **Standard:** A structured packet with expanded execution, milestone, budget, vendor, and editable bid materials.
- **Premium:** A comprehensive governance package for complex projects requiring institutional-level decision-making.

**Illustrative scope points:** Partnership Strategy Outline; Strategic Project Scope Definition; Standardized Bid/Proposal Architecture; Detailed Milestone & Deliverable Mapping; Vendor Comparison Matrix; Partnership Governance Framework.

## Launch & Revenue Ready

**Category:** Launch & Marketing
**Purpose:** Digital launch packages that get businesses online, generating leads, and revenue-ready fast.

**Tiers:**

- **Basic:** A ready-to-launch digital presence designed to convert visitors into documented leads immediately.
- **Standard:** A complete digital launch system with a multi-page website, email automation, and revenue infrastructure.
- **Premium:** A full-stack revenue engine for businesses that need to launch fast and scale immediately.

**Illustrative scope points:** Strategic Launch Kickoff & Discovery; One-Page Custom Framer Website; High-Conversion Lead Capture Architecture; Multi-Page Custom Framer Website; Email Marketing Automation Setup; Advanced CRM & Sales Pipeline Setup.

## The All-In Master Bundle

**Category:** Bundle
**Purpose:** Complete enterprise architecture package combining all six packet groups into one comprehensive engagement.

**Tier:**

- **Premium:** A comprehensive enterprise architecture package covering the business foundation, operating design, launch system, and go-to-market execution.

**Illustrative scope points:** The Model: business concept, IP protection, and foundation documents; The Advantage: business plans, operating design, and execution frameworks; The Deliverable: launch system, revenue infrastructure, and go-to-market execution.
